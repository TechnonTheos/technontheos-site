My story

For 19 years I led operations teams at FedEx Ground, first running pickup and delivery in South Bend, Indiana, then running a package sort in Maple Grove, Minnesota. A sort is a real-time flow system. Thousands of packages enter, get routed, hit bottlenecks, and have to leave on time. When I started studying networking, I recognized it immediately: traffic, paths, congestion, redundancy, and the people and process that keep it all running.

From May to August 2026 I trained in the NetworkChuck Academy CCNA program, showing up for Monday–Friday daily check-ins with Jeremy Cioara (CCIE), Zach Hill and Chuck Keith. I studied 2–3 hours a day around a full-time job and passed the Cisco CCNA on August 28. Since then I've built a working Cisco and UniFi lab at home and designed networks from scratch. I've started automating with Ansible, and I document all of it on this site.

My long-term goal is the CCIE Enterprise Infrastructure. Along the way, I want to help other career changers, people with jobs and families and limited hours, find a realistic path into networking.
